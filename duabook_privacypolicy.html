Privacy Policy for Dhondhooni Dua Book

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Dhondhooni Dua Book mobile application ("App"). We respect the privacy of our users, especially children, and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses).
  • Device & Usage Data: The App runs locally on your device. We do not track device identifiers, location, IP addresses, or app usage statistics.
  • App Data & Preferences: Favorites, progress, or settings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

  • The App does not require children to input any personal details.
  • Standard safety features like a Parent Gate protect parental controls, settings, and external interactions from accidental clicks by young users.
  • The App contains no third-party ad networks, tracking tools, or social media plugins.

3. Third-Party Services

No user data is sold, rented, or shared with third-party analytics or advertising providers.

4. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to external servers, your data remains secure within your device's operating system environment.
